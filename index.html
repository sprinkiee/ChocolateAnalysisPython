Findings

Key Insights

01
Cocoa % vs Rating

The correlation is slightly negative (−0.16). Contrary to popular belief, 100% cocoa chocolate tends to get lower reviews. The sweet spot for high ratings is between 65% and 75% cocoa.

r = −0.16
02
Yearly Progress

A weak positive correlation between review year and rating (r = 0.10) suggests a gradual improvement in chocolate quality — or a gradual decrease in reviewer pickiness — over time.

r = 0.10
03
Best Bean Origins

The highest-rated beans consistently come from Honduras, Guatemala, and Vietnam — regions producing beans with particularly complex flavour profiles.

Bean Origin
04
Top Manufacturer Countries

Countries with the best average manufacturing ratings are Vietnam, Brazil, and Australia, pointing to strong artisan chocolate cultures in these regions.

Manufacturing
